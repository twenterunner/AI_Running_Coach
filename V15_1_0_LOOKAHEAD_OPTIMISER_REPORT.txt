AI Running Coach v15.1.0 · build 50100

DETERMINISTIC SHOE-ENGINE INTELLIGENCE UPGRADE

Architecture
- Retains the authoritative physical-pair ledger, hard eligibility filters, fail-closed validator, Race Day solver, purchase minimizer and immutable snapshot.
- Adds a deterministic bounded beam-search assignment optimizer.
- Search width: 24 states.
- Rolling look-ahead: 42 days.
- Maximum candidates considered per session: 4 safe eligible physical pairs.
- Runner overrides, Race Day assignments, Race Day familiarisation assignments and the canonical Race Day pair are locked during soft optimization.
- Winning window assignments are applied atomically.
- Adds an independent counterfactual critic which challenges each future non-Race-Day purchase by searching for a feasible assignment without that physical pair. Existing portfolio minimization remains the final authority for removal.
- Optimizer diagnostics and critic findings are persisted in the immutable shoe snapshot.

Lexicographic decision order inside the look-ahead optimizer
1. Hard constraints are eligibility gates and cannot be traded.
2. Material session-suitability loss is minimized.
3. Rotation-balance deviation is minimized only among materially equivalent suitability solutions.
4. Unnecessary switching/continuity loss is minimized.
5. Remaining lifecycle is used sensibly as the final tie-break.
The approved 25% weekly share remains a soft target and cannot create a purchase.

Why this is more robust
The former session-by-session greedy choice could make a locally attractive decision without seeing its effect on later sessions. V15.1 evaluates bounded whole-horizon combinations and keeps the best deterministic partial plans. The critic then asks the inverse question for each planned purchase: can the programme remain feasible without it?

Determinism
All candidate ordering and beam ties have stable pair-ID tie-breaks. No random number, neural network, genetic algorithm, remote service or LLM is in the production decision path.
