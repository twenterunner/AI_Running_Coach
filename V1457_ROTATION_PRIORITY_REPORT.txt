AI Running Coach v14.5.7 · build 40507

Implemented two soft optimisation preferences inside the same SESSION SUITABILITY engine.

PURCHASE SPACING PRIORITY
- Keeps the ~12-week / 84-day purchase separation as the stronger objective.
- Allows materially larger shoe-score sacrifices on selected quality sessions where safe.
- Quality-session reassignment limit: up to 18 suitability points.
- Ordinary-session reassignment limit: up to 22 suitability points.
- Quality-session move penalty is deliberately lower than in Quality-session priority.

QUALITY-SESSION PRIORITY
- Protects threshold, intervals, VO2/Fartlek, race-specific work, rehearsals, progression/tempo and key long runs.
- Quality-session reassignment is limited to small suitability losses (up to 5 points).
- Ordinary-session reassignment remains possible (up to 11 points).
- Quality-session purchase-spacing moves receive a much larger optimisation penalty.
- As a result, the planner accepts a shorter-than-12-week purchase gap sooner rather than materially degrading key-session shoe matching.

Both modes retain identical hard rules: availability, lifecycle, retirement, complete assignment, Race Day safety and runner overrides.

The selector is available directly in the Shoes strategy panel and in Settings > Shoe fit context. Changing it invalidates the shoe-plan cache and recalculates footwear only; the running programme is not rebuilt.
