AI Running Coach v15.6.2 — comprehensive shoe photography update

Scope
- Every built-in ASICS shoe family now resolves to a real internet-sourced product photograph.
- Exact generation-specific mappings are preserved where already available.
- If a specific generation does not yet have an exact photo mapping, the app falls back to a representative photograph for that exact ASICS family instead of showing the pictogram.
- Custom/non-ASICS user-entered shoes still use the generic shoe pictogram because the offline app cannot safely identify arbitrary products from text alone.

Built-in families covered
GEL-NIMBUS, GEL-CUMULUS, NOVABLAST, SUPERBLAST, MEGABLAST, SONICBLAST, MAGIC SPEED, METASPEED SKY, METASPEED EDGE, METASPEED RAY, NOOSA TRI, GEL-PULSE, EVORIDE, GLIDERIDE, DYNABLAST, GEL-KAYANO, GT-2000, GT-1000.

Performance safeguards
- Existing lazy-loading and asynchronous image decoding are retained.
- Today recommendation remains eligible for eager loading; other imagery remains lazy.
- Tab render caching from v15.6.0 remains unchanged.

Validation

- JavaScript syntax: pass
- Service-worker syntax: pass

Important visual accuracy note
Where exact historical-generation photography is unavailable in the mapping, the image is explicitly a representative photo of the same ASICS shoe family, not a claim that the displayed colourway or generation is the runner's exact physical pair.
