AI Running Coach v14.4.0 · build 40400
ACTIVE ROTATION / RETIREMENT RESET

ROOT CAUSE IN v14.3.3
The weekly allocator selected only the top 2–3 "principal" pairs and silently ignored other serviceable pairs.
A physical pair could therefore remain available and unretired while receiving 0% mileage for many weeks.
The graph correctly did not show RETIRE for that pair, but the allocation was wrong and produced the long unused/flat state.

NEW HARD RULES
1. Every serviceable physical pair active for a week belongs to the weekly rotation rule.
2. Every active pair must receive at least 25% of that week's shoe mileage.
3. A serviceable pair can no longer be silently excluded because another pair scores higher.
4. Before a week starts, each pair is tested for whether its remaining lifecycle can support at least 25% of that week using whole appropriate sessions.
5. If it cannot, it is planning-retired at its last use and receives × RETIRE.
6. A replacement is introduced immediately when required so the rotation does not drop below two active serviceable pairs while meaningful programme mileage remains.
7. The normal active portfolio target remains 2–3 pairs.
8. Plans with more than three active pairs, fewer than two active pairs, or an active pair below 25% are validation failures.
9. A dedicated Race Day pair is included in the same weekly rotation once available, but its assignment capacity is capped below 250 km pre-race.
10. Future graph series end at retirement; a serviceable pair cannot remain unused for weeks while another pair is purchased and used instead.

GRAPH
- Solid = actual logged mileage.
- Dashed = future assigned mileage.
- No artificial flat extension is added after final use.
- × RETIRE is derived from the authoritative planning-retirement transition, including the new weekly-capacity retirement rule.
- No mileage is assigned after planned retirement.
