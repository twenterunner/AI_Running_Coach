DAILY SHOE CARD + GRAPH ROOT-CAUSE FIX

ROOT CAUSE 1 — DAILY SESSION SHOE TEXT
The v14.3.2 stylesheet targeted every span inside the shoe header. That also matched the shoe icon,
corrupted grid placement and collapsed the recommendation/model text into an extremely narrow column.
This is the exact one-letter-per-line failure shown in the screenshot.

FIX
- Rebuilt the Plan daily-session shoe card markup with explicit semantic classes.
- Recommendation, model name, score, available-shoe name and available-shoe score each have their own class.
- Rebuilt all shoe-score foldout rows with explicit name/score/component classes.
- No generic span/b selector controls these elements.
- All nested shoe foldouts are constrained to the session-card width.
- The superseded v14.3.2 shoe-card CSS block was physically removed.

ROOT CAUSE 2 — FUTURE MILEAGE LOOKED SOLID
The forecast was drawn as many individual SVG line elements, one per workout.
SVG restarts the dash pattern for every line. Because individual workout segments are short,
each one began with a dash and the combined forecast visually appeared solid.

FIX
- Every physical pair is now drawn as one continuous SVG polyline.
- SOLID = actual logged mileage only.
- DASHED = future assignment-ledger mileage only.
- Owned pairs transition from solid actual history to dashed forecast at Today.

RETIREMENT
- A projected pair is marked retired when its remaining lifecycle cannot fit any remaining programme session,
  rather than requiring an exact numerical hit on the retirement target.
- × RETIRE is plotted at that final usable point.
- Manually retired historical pairs also receive × RETIRE at their last actual mileage point.
- No mileage is plotted after genuine retirement.

RACE DAY
- The selected Race Day pair continues to the Race Day x-position.
- Its dashed curve includes the race itself.
- The Race Day marker shows start-of-race mileage, which remains the value used for the <250 km eligibility rule.
