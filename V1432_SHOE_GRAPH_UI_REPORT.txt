AI Running Coach v14.3.2 · build 40302
SHOE GRAPH + DAILY SESSION UI CORRECTION

1. DAILY SESSION SHOE TEXT SPACING
The complete shoe section is now a single vertical layout boundary. The recommendation header,
AVAILABLE SHOES rows, comparison foldout, Why this shoe and action button can no longer inherit
side-by-side Plan-tab grid positioning.

The recommendation row has a dedicated score column and each available-shoe row has its own fixed
score column, preventing the overlapping text shown in the supplied screenshot.

2. AUTHORITATIVE GRAPH SEMANTICS
The graph now has two explicitly separate data sources:
- SOLID = shoeMileageSeries() from actual logged/manual historical shoe usage only.
- DASHED = final future physical-pair assignment ledger only.

Owned shoes therefore transition from solid actual mileage at TODAY to dashed forecast mileage after TODAY.

3. RACE DAY PAIR REACHES RACE DAY
The selected Race Day physical pair now receives an explicit Race Day forecast point.
Race Day itself is a real mileage-producing event, so the dashed usage curve reaches Race Day and adds
the race distance there.

A separate marker at the Race Day x-position shows START-OF-RACE mileage. This is the mileage used
for the <250 km Race Day eligibility rule. The plotted curve itself continues through the race distance.

4. RETIREMENT
A genuine retirement is rendered as an explicit × RETIRE marker at the pair's retirement point.
The physical-pair series ends there; no forecast mileage is drawn after retirement.

5. WHY MEGABLAST WAS BEING CHOSEN
The previous dedicated Race Day purchase selector evaluated candidates across a 56-day training horizon.
That favored versatile race-capable trainers such as MEGABLAST because they scored strongly across easy,
long and tempo training as well as racing.

That was the wrong objective for a purchase whose sole purpose is Race Day.

v14.3.2 separates the objectives:
- an ordinary future training purchase is still selected against the upcoming training block;
- a dedicated Race Day purchase is scored 82% on Race Day suitability for the actual race distance and
  runner context, and only 18% on suitability for familiarisation sessions.

MEGABLAST may still be selected as an ordinary future training pair if it genuinely wins the programme-wide
training score. It will no longer win a dedicated Race Day purchase merely because it is the most versatile trainer.
