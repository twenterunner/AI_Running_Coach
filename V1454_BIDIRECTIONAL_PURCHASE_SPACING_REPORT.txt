AI Running Coach v14.5.4 · build 40504

PURCHASE-SPACING OPTIMISATION

Preferred purchase separation remains 12 weeks / 84 days and remains a soft target.

New bidirectional behaviour:
1. First attempt to delay the later purchase by reallocating its early sessions.
2. If that cannot reach the target, restore the original plan and try to bring the earlier purchase forward.
3. The earlier pair is introduced on a real suitable session, not an artificial purchase-only date.
4. Additional early mileage is assigned so the earlier purchase represents meaningful use.
5. Lower-value sessions are preferred.
6. Race-specific/quality sessions carry a high suitability-loss penalty but are not absolutely excluded; they may be compromised when required to materially improve purchase spacing.
7. The optimiser avoids pulling a purchase so early that it creates a new <12-week conflict with the purchase before it.
8. If the full 84-day target remains impossible, the engine keeps a genuine improvement where possible and explains the remaining exception.

The 84-day spacing target does not override hard lifecycle, shoe availability, runner override or Race Day safety boundaries.
