AI Running Coach v14.5.9 · build 40509

ROOT ISSUE
The planner could improve calendar purchase spacing by assigning a token early session to a future pair and then
leave that pair largely unused until its original need date. This created a long near-flat mileage line and made
the two rotation preferences converge.

PURCHASE SPACING PRIORITY
- An earlier purchase is accepted only if the pair becomes a genuine active rotation member.
- From first use until the original need date, target >=25% of every multi-session week's shoe mileage.
- If this sustained-use target cannot be achieved within lifecycle/suitability/override rules, the purchase is not advanced.
- Selected quality-session compromise remains allowed within the safe suitability boundary.

QUALITY-SESSION PRIORITY
- The engine does not bring an earlier purchase forward merely to improve the 12-week calendar gap.
- It can still delay the later purchase using lower-priority mileage where feasible.
- Key quality/race-specific matching remains protected.
- A shorter-than-12-week purchase gap is accepted sooner.

EXPECTED VISUAL DIFFERENCE
Purchase-spacing priority may show an earlier BUY 1, but its mileage curve must then continue rising through regular use.
Quality-session priority should generally leave BUY 1 at its natural need date and can therefore show a shorter purchase gap.
