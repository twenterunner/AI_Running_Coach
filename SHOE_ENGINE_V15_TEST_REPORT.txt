RECURRENCE PREVENTION
The previous inconsistency class is prevented by publishing one validated snapshot from the final ledger. Invalid candidates are not cached. The fingerprint makes repeated identical calculations stable while invalidating on shoe-relevant data changes. Purchases, assignments, graph points, retirements, Race Day data and explanations are captured from the same final physical-pair solution rather than independently recomputed for display.
