Model and metric definitions
Efficiency factor · J/heartbeat

Average power × 60 ÷ average heart rate. It estimates external running work per heartbeat. Compare only similar conditions and run types.

Power-based cardiac drift · %

Change in power per heartbeat from the first half to the second half after warm-up. Lower is better and timestamped heart-rate and power data are required.

Evidence-based coaching assessment

Verification standard

The coach uses only configured goals, generated plan data, logged runs, valid assessments, Garmin HRV and pain entries. Missing information is treated as missing rather than guessed. Model-calibrated predictions are labelled separately from directly observed evidence.

How the adaptive rehabilitation coach works
Daily prescription

Exercises are selected from the current stage and broad injury location. Dosage is guidance, not a substitute for an individual clinical programme.

Trajectory comparison

Your recovery score combines pain, stiffness, walking, function, confidence, impact and next-morning response, then compares it with the original expectation.

Criteria before dates

Stages advance when functional and symptom-response criteria are met. A date alone never clears running or sprinting.

Clinical boundary

The app does not diagnose injury grade or medical readiness. Severe, worsening, recurrent or persistent symptoms require assessment by a qualified clinician.
