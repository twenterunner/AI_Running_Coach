How to interpret the model

The confidence score combines fitness, endurance, consistency, adherence, recovery and economy. A strong test result cannot fully compensate for limited long-run endurance or poor training execution. The coach therefore ranks the weakest evidence-weighted components rather than reacting to one isolated number.
