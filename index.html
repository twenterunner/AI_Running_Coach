Model and metric definitions
Efficiency factor · J/heartbeat

Average power × 60 ÷ average heart rate. It estimates external running work per heartbeat. Compare only similar conditions and run types.

Power-based cardiac drift · %

Change in power per heartbeat from the first half to the second half after warm-up. Lower is better and timestamped heart-rate and power data are required.

How to rate RPE and pain, and how Garmin HRV is used
RPE 1–10

1–2: very easy · 3–4: easy/conversational · 5–6: moderate · 7: marathon effort · 8: threshold · 9: very hard · 10: maximal.

Pain 0–10

0: none · 1–2: awareness only · 3–4: mild discomfort · 5–6: affects mechanics · 7–10: stop or seek assessment.

Garmin HRV

Enter the previous night’s Garmin HRV in milliseconds. The app starts learning from the first value, compares the recent trend with your personal baseline and gradually increases its influence as more values are collected. HRV affects future training adaptation, not the central race prediction.
