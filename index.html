Metric definitions
Efficiency factor · J/heartbeat

Average power × 60 ÷ average heart rate. It estimates the joules of external running work produced per heartbeat. Higher is better when comparing similar conditions and run types.

Power-based cardiac drift · %

The percentage deterioration in power per heartbeat from the first half to the second half after warm-up. Lower is better. It requires timestamped heart-rate and power data.

How to rate RPE, pain and recovery
RPE 1–10

1–2: very easy · 3–4: easy/conversational · 5–6: moderate · 7: marathon effort · 8: threshold · 9: very hard · 10: maximal.

Pain 0–10

0: none · 1–2: awareness only · 3–4: mild discomfort · 5–6: affects mechanics · 7–10: stop or seek assessment.

Recovery 1–5

1: very poor, heavily fatigued · 2: poor, noticeable fatigue · 3: normal, ready for planned training · 4: good, recovered quickly · 5: excellent, exceptionally fresh.

How to interpret the model

The confidence score combines fitness, endurance, consistency, adherence, recovery and economy. A strong test result cannot fully compensate for limited long-run endurance or poor training execution. The coach therefore ranks the weakest evidence-weighted components rather than reacting to one isolated number.
