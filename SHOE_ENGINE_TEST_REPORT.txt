AI RUNNING COACH — SHOE ENGINE CORRECTIVE RELEASE TEST REPORT
Release: v14.0.4 · build 40004

NEW RULES
- Every available ordinary training shoe must receive at least 25% of that week's running mileage whenever this is feasible with indivisible planned sessions, lifecycle capacity and acceptable session fit.
- Manual shoe overrides remain authoritative.
- Dedicated Race Day shoes are excluded from the ordinary 25% floor; they have a separate race-familiarisation requirement.
- A future dedicated Race Day shoe must have at least one positive-mileage exposure inside the final 42 days before the race, while retaining the existing minimum familiarisation and <250 km Race Day ceiling.
- Race familiarisation candidates are now selected from 42/56/70-day horizons and prioritise the final 42 days, race-relevant sessions and later dates.

WHY THE PREVIOUS GRAPH WAS WRONG
The previous optimiser only tried to accumulate enough familiarisation mileage somewhere in a broad 70/84/112-day window. It did not require an exposure close to Race Day. That allowed a race shoe to accumulate its minimum mileage early and then disappear from the graph until the race. This release makes that invalid.
