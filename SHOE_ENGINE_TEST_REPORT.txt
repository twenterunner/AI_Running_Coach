1. FUTURE-PAIR FIRST-USE CHRONOLOGY
The assignment ledger is now authoritative for future-pair timing.

produce repeated false future-pair-mileage-before-first-use failures.

0–100 suitability score. The selected pair is marked with a check.

Today and rehabilitation surfaces use the same authoritative ranking.
