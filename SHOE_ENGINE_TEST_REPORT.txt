AI RUNNING COACH — SHOE ENGINE CORRECTIVE RELEASE TEST REPORT
Release: v14.0.5 · build 40005

FIX
v14.0.4 incorrectly promoted the 25% weekly rotation target to a hard lifecycle invariant.
Because sessions are indivisible, an exact 25% split is not always mathematically possible. This caused an otherwise
valid shoe lifecycle to be suppressed and replaced by repeated available-shoe-below-25pct-weekly-mileage errors.

v14.0.5:
- keeps the 25% weekly target in the allocation optimiser;
- reallocates whole sessions toward >=25% per available ordinary pair when feasible;
- preserves stronger constraints: session appropriateness, manual overrides, retirement/capacity and Race Day rules;
- treats a residual <25% share as an advisory when whole-session arithmetic or stronger constraints prevent exact compliance;
- never suppresses an otherwise valid graph merely because the weekly percentage cannot be hit exactly;
- retains the final-42-day Race Day shoe familiarisation requirement.
