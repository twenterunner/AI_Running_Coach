CORRECTION
- Removed the user-facing "Shoe plan temporarily blocked" release state.
- Added lifecycleRepairPlan() between BUILD and final VALIDATE.
- Sequential future replacements are no longer incorrectly counted as simultaneously maintained shoes.
- Eight-week retirement spacing is advisory unless preventability can actually be established; it cannot itself block a valid plan.
- Future purchase dates are repaired to the first mileage-producing use.
- Future race pairs that cannot be adequately familiarised are rejected in favour of an appropriate owned race-capable pair when available.
- Authoritative mileage/graph points are rebuilt from the assignment ledger before final validation.
- Session selection is score-driven: no idle-time or retirement-spacing bonus can make a lower-scoring shoe win.

  Responsiveness/efficiency, Stability/support, Surface/grip, Foot-mechanics context,
  Rehabilitation context where relevant, and Lifecycle/condition.
