ROOT CAUSE
The dedicated Race Day pair was being added too late and then familiarised by moving a cluster of late
sessions onto it. Those late moves could satisfy Race Day mileage while simultaneously breaking the weekly
>=25% share of another active pair. Earlier versions then alternated between:
- active-pair-below-25pct / fewer-than-two-active-pairs; and
- no safe Race Day shoe.

CORRECTION
1. Existing owned/planned pairs are considered for Race Day only when their normal programme usage already
   provides enough familiarity, <250 km at race start, suitable race fit and enough lifecycle.
2. If a dedicated Race Day pair is required, its entry date is calculated backwards from Race Day using the
   normal weekly >=25% rotation rule rather than by cramming familiarisation sessions at the end.
3. The Race Day pair is integrated into the ordinary weekly optimiser from its first-use week.
4. No late post-processing mileage-forcing pass is allowed afterwards.
5. This keeps all active physical pairs inside the same weekly rotation rules.
