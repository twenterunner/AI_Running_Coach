AI Running Coach — Shoe Engine Availability Boundary Fix
Release: v14.2.2 · build 40202

ROOT CAUSE
The planner had a final reconciliation step that changed a future physical pair's purchase/availability
date to the earliest assignment found in its ledger. If an earlier session had been incorrectly moved onto
that future pair, this step moved the shoe's availability backwards and made the illegal assignment look valid.

FIX
- Every future physical pair receives an immutable plannedEntryDate when it is created.
- shoePlannerCanUse() rejects sessions before that date.
- shoePlannerAssign() rejects sessions before that date.
- shoePlannerMove() rejects reassignment before that date BEFORE mutating either pair.
- Weekly rebalancing, lifecycle repair and Race Day familiarisation all pass through the same availability boundary.
- Final lifecycle rebuild no longer moves purchase/availability dates backwards.
- Validation checks every future assignment against immutable plannedEntryDate.
- The graph starts a future pair only at immutable plannedEntryDate.
- Owned shoes remain available according to their real physical inventory state.
