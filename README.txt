v8.5.3 PREDICTED-READINESS SCOPE CORRECTION

Training-day availability is now treated only as a forward-looking constraint.

CURRENT READINESS
- The current readiness percentage is calculated from evidence already achieved: fitness, completed endurance, efficiency, plan execution, recovery/health, preparation time, completed long runs and completed race-specific work.
- Changing the number of available training days does not rewrite current fitness or completed evidence.
- Training Opportunity is therefore excluded from the current-readiness pillars and from the current overall percentage.

PREDICTED RACE-DAY READINESS
- The science-based Training Opportunity model remains active.
- Enabled days, race distance, phase and planned peak weekly volume determine the forward-looking opportunity penalty.
- This penalty applies only to predicted race-day readiness and its associated status.
- Reducing future training days therefore lowers the forecast immediately without incorrectly lowering the athlete's present condition.

DATA COMPATIBILITY
The persistent schema remains 8500. Existing settings, plans, runs, assessments and backups remain compatible.
